I’m currently a Full Professor of Organizational Behavior at Oslo Metropolitan University.

I completed my PhD in Organizational Behavior at BI Norwegian Business School in 2023, supervised by Professor Thorvald Hærem and Associate Professor Erik Løhre. My dissertation examined the role of emotions and psychological distance in decision-making under risk and uncertainty. During my PhD, I was also a visiting scholar at New York University, with [Trope Lab](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/tropelab/). I then completed a post-doctoral fellowship in Behavioral Economics at [JEDI lab](http://jedilab.weebly.com/) at Linköping University.

My research covers different themes, but broadly, it centers on judgment and decision-making, focusing on how emotions and various cognitive and social psychological factors shape people’s decisions, particularly those made under risk and uncertainty.
